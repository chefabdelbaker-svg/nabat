The house

A bakery born of a road.

They built no empires. They built roads.

They conquered no peoples. They connected them.

They were called the Nabataeans. Their spirit became ours.

At NABATE we own nothing: not the bread, which began thousands of years before us, nor the coffee, born in Ethiopia and carried through Yemen. What belongs to us is the link between these stories. NABATE is the bridge, the keeper of the halt on the road.

The caravan never stops. It only rests.

Two ambassadors

Bread and coffee, two languages of one house.

Bread

The memory of the earth

The sourdough fed every day, the crust that crackles, the crumb still warm. The moment bread is torn is prepared hours before you arrive.

Coffee

The road in a cup

Ethiopia gave the bean, Yemen the roast, Arabia the qahwa. Offering coffee is the oldest gesture of hospitality here, and the most contemporary.

The compass

Four lines, one philosophy of time.

  1. 01

    The past is the foundation

    The history of bread and of roads gives depth to every product. It is felt in the taste, never staged.

  2. 02

    The Nabataeans are the spark

    Builders of links, travellers, carriers of grain and know-how. Their spirit inspires NABATE; it is not its museum.

  3. 03

    Time is the thread

    The long time of the craft, the human time of the encounter. We respect both and oppose neither.

  4. 04

    Bread and coffee are the ambassadors

    Bread is memory and anchor. Coffee is journey and encounter. Together they tell the whole of NABATE.

The chef

Abdelhafid Arbi

Chef entrepreneur and international consultant

Twenty years in the trade. A French-trained baker and pastry chef who has built, trained and advised, in France and abroad, notably in Uruguay and Saudi Arabia. NABATE carries that experience: demanding in the dough, generous at the table.

20years of craft
5+countries of consulting
2019World Pain au Chocolat Championship
  • Trained in France at the INBP: CAP Boulanger, CAP Pâtissier, CQP Tourier.
  • Trained under Meilleur Ouvrier de France Olivier Magne.
  • Selected for the 2019 World Pain au Chocolat Championship.
  • Took part in opening four Croi Bakehouse shops and in training more than 150 team members.
  • Founded a bakery in Uruguay, today four branches.
  • Consulting missions in France, Uruguay, Morocco, Bahrain, Algeria and Saudi Arabia.
