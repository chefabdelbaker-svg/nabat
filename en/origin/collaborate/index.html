Method

From an idea to a product ready to produce.

  1. Brief

    Your concept, your customers, your constraints and your target cost.

  2. Tasting and prototypes

    Recipes tested together until the result is the right one.

  3. Costing

    Yield, raw materials, labour and packaging: a clear cost per piece.

  4. Production trial

    A first batch on the real line, measured against the prototype.

  5. Launch and follow-up

    Regular deliveries, quality checks and adjustments over time.
