The House

Origin, quality, gesture.

Maison NABATE is an artisan house of French training, born in Riyadh. Here is what it is made of.

Origin

A name that means to grow.

NABATE comes from the Arabic root ن-ب-ت: to sprout, to grow, to pass on. The Nabataeans, builders of roads and links, inspire us. They are a source, never a decoration.

Quality

Quality begins with what we choose.

Butter, flour, eggs, milk, chocolate, coffee: reference houses, constant quality, traced lots. We never compromise on the raw material, because nothing can fix it afterwards.

The chef

A chef at the helm.

Abdelhafid Arbi: twenty years in the trade, trained in France at the INBP. Recipes, standards and quality checks start with the chef, then are written down so the house never depends on a single day.

Meet the chef

The laboratory

What happens inside.

Mechanise the process, keep the gesture. Four steps, each one written and checked.

01

Selection and weighing

Every ingredient is chosen, weighed and traced before each batch.

02

Fermentation and lamination

Time and the French gesture: slow doughs, patient lamination.

03

Baking, set to the detail

Baking programmes defined product by product, so the tenth batch matches the first.

04

Finishing and packing

Each reference leaves finished, packed and ready to serve.
